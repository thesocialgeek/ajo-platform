FAQ

Before you join.

Is my preferred collection date guaranteed?

No. It helps us understand what you need. Your collection date becomes confirmed only after we approve your group and position.

Does the person collecting still pay the service fee?

Yes. The collector skips the contribution for their collection month, but the service fee is payable by every member each month.

Is this an investment?

No. Ajo 2.0 is designed around rotating contributions and collection turns. It is not marketed as an investment and does not promise investment returns.

What happens after I apply?

We review your details and preferred timing. If a suitable position is available and you meet the requirements, we contact you to confirm your group, position and next steps.
