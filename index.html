FAQ

Before you join.

What happens when I choose a position?

Submitting your application holds that live position so another applicant cannot choose it. The position becomes confirmed after your application is approved. If your application is not approved, the position is released.

Does the person collecting still pay the service fee?

Yes. The collector skips the contribution for their collection month, but the service fee is payable by every member each month.

Is this an investment?

No. Ajo 2.0 is designed around rotating contributions and collection turns. It is not marketed as an investment and does not promise investment returns.

What happens after I apply?

Your chosen position is held immediately. We review your application, then confirm your membership and collection turn if approved. You can follow the process using your application reference.
